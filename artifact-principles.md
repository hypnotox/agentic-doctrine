# Artifact principles

These principles specialize the [working principles](working-principles.md) for creating and maintaining documents and other lasting outputs.

## 1. Let purpose determine form.

Shape the artifact around its intended use and audience. Let that purpose determine its content, organization, and level of detail.

## 2. Preserve meaning and necessary context.

Include the context and reasoning needed to interpret and use the artifact correctly. Prefer useful synthesis to exhaustive reproduction.

## 3. Make the status of the content clear.

Distinguish proposals, decisions, and observations where the difference matters. Make unresolved or uncertain content apparent.

## 4. Let structure serve the content.

Choose organization and presentation that help the reader find and use what matters. Adapt templates to the content rather than creating content to fill them.

## 5. Keep artifacts useful over time.

Maintain information that is meant to stay current. Preserve history when it remains useful, without leaving superseded material looking current.
